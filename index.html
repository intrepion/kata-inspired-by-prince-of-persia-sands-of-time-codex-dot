FIELD NOTES

Move with A / D or ← / →. Space jumps; X slashes; Shift guards; R rewinds; E interacts. P / Escape pauses. T returns to your saved anchor.

How to survive the repeating night

AcrobaticsJump toward a wall, then jump again to kick away. Hold toward a wall to slide slowly. Catch a lip automatically; press jump to climb.

CombatFace your foe. Slash within a blade's reach. Gold means a strike is coming. Hold guard, or start guarding just before the strike to parry.

Time & anchorsHold rewind to spend up to six seconds of reserve. Release to choose a new future. Use cyan anchors to refill and save. Reload resumes at the last anchor, not mid-jump.

Keyboard & touchButtons can be held and combined. Jump, slash and use fire once per press. Losing focus pauses and releases controls. Audio starts with your journey and can be muted.

Progress saves on this browser at anchors and chamber transitions.
