# The Last Meridian
A stormbound city is held in one repeating night. Its courier must restore the meridian bell.

## Language
**Courier**: Sera, the playable keeper of the borrowed minute.
**Reserve**: The finite seconds available for reversing the recent past in the original adventure. _Avoid_: mana.
**Thread**: The recent sequence of events that the courier can unwind. _Avoid_: ghost.
**Anchor**: A safe resting point that restores vitality and time resources, and records progress.
**Sentinel**: A clockwork defender with a visible preparation before each strike.
**Meridian**: The bell whose restoration ends the repeating night.

**Sand tank**: One charge of the borrowed minute, spent on a continuous rewind and replenished by retrieving a defeated sentinel’s sand. _Avoid_: reserve seconds.
**Knockdown**: A sentinel’s temporary defeat before its sand is retrieved; an unfinished sentinel can rise again. _Avoid_: kill.
**Courtyard route**: The path across the broken aqueduct, high gallery and bell court.
